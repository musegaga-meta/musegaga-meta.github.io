Privacy Policy

This application is used privately by its owner to access and manage Google Drive files using rclone and Nextcloud.

The application accesses Google Drive data to list, read, create, modify, and delete files.

Data is processed through the owner's self-hosted server. OAuth credentials are stored privately on that server.

Google Drive data is not sold or shared with third parties for advertising purposes.

The application follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

This website does not intentionally collect personal information.
